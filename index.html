Who We Are

Cabrera Elite Baseball Development is led by a coaching staff with real professional scouting and strength & conditioning experience — not just former players, but people who have signed and developed talent that reached affiliated professional baseball.

Founder & Project Leader

Martín Cabrera

Former professional scout with 18 years of experience working for the Colorado Rockies organization. Over that career, he signed and developed numerous prospects who went on to reach affiliated professional baseball.

Today he is fully focused on Cabrera Elite Baseball Development, a project built to help young athletes grow and reach their full potential in their athletic careers through structured, day-to-day training.

Five pillars of development

Every athlete works through the same five areas, in different proportions depending on position, age, and goals.

01

Hitting Mechanics

Swing breakdown, timing, and reps against live and machine pitching.

02

Pitching Development

Mechanics, command, and arm-care built into every throwing day.

03

Infield & Outfield

Footwork, first-step reaction, and game-speed defensive reps.

04

Strength & Conditioning

Age-appropriate strength, speed, and injury-prevention work.

05

Game IQ

Situational reps and film so athletes think the game, not just play it.
