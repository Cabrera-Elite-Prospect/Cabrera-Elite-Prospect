How it works, step by step

  1. Press “Open my account”.A secure Stripe page opens. It is not our website, and that is normal.
  2. Type the email you used to register.It must be the same email you used when you paid.
  3. Check your inbox for a code.Stripe emails you a one-time code. If you do not see it, look in spam or promotions.
  4. Enter the code.That is your sign-in. There is no password to remember.
  5. Choose what you need.Update your card, download a receipt or cancel. Changes apply right away.

Common questions

I did not receive the code

Check spam or promotions and make sure you typed the same email you used to pay. If it still does not arrive, email info@cabreraelite.com with the athlete's name and we will help you.

When am I charged?

Monthly plans renew automatically on the same date each month, at the price shown when you signed up. To avoid the next charge, cancel before your next billing date.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep your sessions until the end of the month you already paid. Unused sessions do not roll over, and all sales are final. See our Refund Policy.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Email us at info@cabreraelite.com or call +1 (863) 268-9210 and we will change it for you.

I paid for a single practice

Single practices are one-time payments, so there is nothing to manage. Your receipt was emailed to you right after payment.

Is it safe?

Yes. Your card details are handled only by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
